Zinc

Overview

Zinc is an important industrial metal used in galvanizing, alloys, construction, manufacturing, batteries, and other industrial applications.

AMMNIR MINES supports the sourcing and trading of zinc-bearing mineral materials from Nigeria for qualified buyers.

Our Zinc Activities

We support:

- Zinc ore sourcing
- Supplier coordination
- Buyer requirements
- Material specification verification
- Sampling and inspection arrangements
- Quantity coordination
- Logistics and delivery coordination
- Local and international trade opportunities

Quality & Specifications

Zinc-bearing materials are evaluated according to the specifications agreed with the buyer.

Important considerations may include:

- Zinc content
- Mineral composition
- Moisture content
- Impurities
- Quantity
- Sampling and laboratory analysis

Final specifications and acceptance criteria are agreed between the buyer and supplier before a transaction is completed.

Supply Opportunities

AMMNIR MINES works with qualified suppliers and buyers interested in zinc mineral supply opportunities in Nigeria.

Availability, grade, quantity, location, pricing, inspection, and delivery terms are confirmed for each transaction.

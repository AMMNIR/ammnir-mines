Our Services

AMMNIR MINES provides mineral sourcing, trading, procurement, and supply solutions for local and international markets.

1. Mineral Sourcing

We identify and source mineral commodities from reliable suppliers and producers across Nigeria.

Our sourcing process focuses on:

- Supplier verification
- Material specifications
- Quantity requirements
- Location and logistics
- Commercial terms

2. Mineral Trading

We facilitate the buying and selling of mineral commodities based on agreed specifications, quantities, and commercial terms.

3. Mineral Procurement

We help buyers identify suitable mineral supplies according to their required:

- Mineral type
- Grade and purity
- Quantity
- Delivery location
- Quality requirements

4. Quality & Specification Verification

Before transactions are completed, material specifications can be evaluated according to the requirements agreed between the buyer and supplier.

This may include sampling, laboratory testing, inspection, and documentation where required.

5. Logistics & Delivery Coordination

We coordinate with suppliers, transporters, warehouses, and buyers to support the movement and delivery of mineral commodities.

6. Supplier & Buyer Coordination

We connect qualified suppliers with potential buyers and help coordinate communication, specifications, inspections, negotiations, and delivery arrangements.

7. Strategic Partnerships

AMMNIR MINES welcomes partnerships with:

- Mining companies
- Mineral suppliers
- Processors
- Commodity traders
- International buyers
- Logistics providers
- Strategic investors and business partners

Our Commitment

We aim to build reliable mineral supply chains based on quality, transparency, professionalism, and long-term business relationships.

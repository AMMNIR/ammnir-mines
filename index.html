About AMMNIR MINES

Reliable mineral sourcing and commodity trading

AMMNIR MINES is a Nigeria-based mining and mineral trading company focused on the sourcing, procurement, trading and supply of mineral commodities.

We work to connect reliable mineral suppliers with qualified buyers while focusing on quality, transparency, responsible sourcing and dependable supply.

Our goal is to build long-term relationships with suppliers, processors, manufacturers and international commodity buyers.

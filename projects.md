AMMNIR MINES — Projects

Overview

AMMNIR MINES is developing mineral sourcing and trading opportunities across Nigeria and international markets.

Our projects focus on building reliable supply channels between mineral suppliers, processors, traders, and qualified buyers.

Current Areas of Focus

Lithium Supply

Sourcing and trading lithium-bearing materials according to agreed buyer specifications, grades, quantities, and delivery requirements.

Tantalite Supply

Developing reliable sourcing channels for tantalite-bearing materials and connecting qualified suppliers with buyers.

Lead & Zinc Supply

Building supply opportunities for lead and zinc-bearing mineral materials for qualified local and international buyers.

Fluorite Supply

Exploring reliable fluorite sourcing opportunities based on buyer specifications and required grades.

Zircon Supply

Developing sourcing and trading opportunities for zircon-bearing materials for industrial and international markets.

Project Development

Our approach includes:

- Identifying reliable mineral sources
- Verifying suppliers
- Confirming material specifications
- Coordinating sampling and laboratory testing
- Connecting with qualified buyers
- Negotiating commercial terms
- Coordinating logistics and delivery

Future Opportunities

AMMNIR MINES aims to expand its mineral trading network and develop long-term relationships with suppliers, processors, investors, logistics providers, and international buyers.

Partnerships

We welcome strategic partnerships that can strengthen mineral sourcing, processing, logistics, trading, and international market access.

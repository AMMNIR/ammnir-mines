Fluorite

Overview

Fluorite is an important industrial mineral used in the production of hydrofluoric acid, aluminum, steel, chemicals, glass, ceramics, and other industrial products.

AMMNIR MINES supports the sourcing and trading of fluorite-bearing mineral materials from Nigeria for qualified buyers.

Our Fluorite Activities

We support:

- Fluorite mineral sourcing
- Supplier coordination
- Buyer requirements
- Material specification verification
- Sampling and inspection arrangements
- Quantity coordination
- Logistics and delivery coordination
- Local and international trade opportunities

Quality & Specifications

Fluorite materials are evaluated according to the specifications agreed with the buyer.

Important considerations may include:

- Calcium fluoride (CaF₂) content
- Mineral composition
- Moisture content
- Impurities
- Quantity
- Sampling and laboratory analysis

Final specifications and acceptance criteria are agreed between the buyer and supplier before a transaction is completed.

Supply Opportunities

AMMNIR MINES works with qualified suppliers and buyers interested in fluorite supply opportunities in Nigeria.

Availability, grade, quantity, location, pricing, inspection, and delivery terms are confirmed for each transaction.

Lithium

Overview

Lithium is an important industrial mineral used in energy storage, batteries, ceramics, glass, and other industrial applications.

AMMNIR MINES is involved in the sourcing and trading of lithium-bearing mineral materials from Nigeria for qualified buyers.

Our Lithium Activities

We support:

- Lithium mineral sourcing
- Supplier coordination
- Buyer requirements
- Material specification verification
- Quantity coordination
- Logistics and delivery arrangements
- Local and international trade opportunities

Quality & Specifications

Lithium materials are evaluated according to the buyer's required specifications.

Depending on the transaction, relevant factors may include:

- Lithium grade
- Mineral composition
- Moisture content
- Impurities
- Quantity
- Sampling and laboratory analysis

Final specifications and acceptance criteria are agreed between the buyer and supplier before a transaction is completed.

Supply Opportunities

AMMNIR MINES works with qualified suppliers and buyers interested in lithium mineral supply opportunities in Nigeria.

Supply availability, grade, quantity, location, pricing, inspection, and delivery terms are confirmed on a transaction-by-transaction basis.

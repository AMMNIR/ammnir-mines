Lead

Overview

Lead is an important industrial metal used in batteries, construction materials, radiation shielding, manufacturing, and other industrial applications.

AMMNIR MINES supports the sourcing and trading of lead-bearing mineral materials from Nigeria for qualified buyers.

Our Lead Activities

We support:

- Lead ore sourcing
- Supplier coordination
- Buyer requirements
- Material specification verification
- Sampling and inspection arrangements
- Quantity coordination
- Logistics and delivery coordination
- Local and international trade opportunities

Quality & Specifications

Lead-bearing materials are evaluated according to the specifications agreed with the buyer.

Important considerations may include:

- Lead content
- Mineral composition
- Moisture content
- Impurities
- Quantity
- Sampling and laboratory analysis

Final specifications and acceptance criteria are agreed between the buyer and supplier before a transaction is completed.

Supply Opportunities

AMMNIR MINES works with qualified suppliers and buyers interested in lead mineral supply opportunities in Nigeria.

Availability, grade, quantity, location, pricing, inspection, and delivery terms are confirmed for each transaction.

- Strategic business partners

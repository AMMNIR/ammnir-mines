Zircon

Overview

Zircon is a naturally occurring mineral and an important source of zirconium. It is used in ceramics, foundry applications, refractories, abrasives, and other industrial applications.

AMMNIR MINES supports the sourcing and trading of zircon-bearing mineral materials from Nigeria for qualified buyers.

Our Zircon Activities

We support:

- Zircon mineral sourcing
- Supplier coordination
- Buyer requirements
- Material specification verification
- Sampling and inspection arrangements
- Quantity coordination
- Logistics and delivery coordination
- Local and international trade opportunities

Quality & Specifications

Zircon materials are evaluated according to the specifications agreed with the buyer.

Important considerations may include:

- Zircon (ZrSiO₄) content
- Zirconium content
- Mineral composition
- Moisture content
- Impurities
- Quantity
- Sampling and laboratory analysis

Final specifications and acceptance criteria are agreed between the buyer and supplier before a transaction is completed.

Supply Opportunities

AMMNIR MINES works with qualified suppliers and buyers interested in zircon supply opportunities in Nigeria.

Availability, grade, quantity, location, pricing, inspection, and delivery terms are confirmed for each transaction.

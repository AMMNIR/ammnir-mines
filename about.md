About AMMNIR MINES

Company Overview

AMMNIR MINES is a Nigeria-based mining and mineral trading company focused on the sourcing, procurement, trading, and supply of mineral commodities.

We work with mineral suppliers, traders, processors, and buyers to facilitate reliable and transparent mineral supply opportunities.

What We Do

Our business activities include:

- Mineral sourcing and procurement
- Mineral trading and supply
- Supplier and buyer coordination
- Mineral specification verification
- Logistics and delivery coordination
- Commercial supply arrangements
- Strategic business partnerships

Our Focus

AMMNIR MINES focuses on commercially valuable mineral commodities sourced from Nigeria and supplied to qualified local and international buyers.

Our areas of interest include lithium, tantalite, lead, zinc, fluorite, zircon, and other mineral commodities.

Our Mission

To build a reliable and transparent mineral supply network connecting Nigerian mineral resources with local and international markets.

Our Vision

To become a trusted African mineral trading and supply company recognized for reliability, professionalism, quality, and long-term business relationships.

Our Values

Integrity — We believe in honest and transparent business dealings.

Reliability — We aim to deliver according to agreed specifications and commercial terms.

Quality — We prioritize proper verification and agreed material specifications.

Partnership — We build long-term relationships with suppliers, buyers, and strategic partners.

Professionalism — We approach every transaction with clear communication and responsible business practices.

Partnerships

AMMNIR MINES is open to working with qualified:

- Mineral suppliers
- Mining companies
- Processors
- Commodity traders
- International buyers
- Logistics companies
- Strategic business partners

AMMNIR MINES — Logistics & Delivery

Overview

AMMNIR MINES coordinates logistics and delivery arrangements for mineral commodities sourced from Nigeria.

Our goal is to support the safe, efficient, and organized movement of mineral materials from suppliers to agreed delivery locations.

Logistics Services

We coordinate:

- Mineral transportation
- Loading arrangements
- Warehouse delivery
- Supplier-to-buyer transportation
- Delivery scheduling
- Logistics partner coordination
- Documentation and transaction coordination

Delivery Process

1. Confirm mineral type, grade, and quantity.
2. Confirm supplier and loading location.
3. Agree on delivery destination.
4. Arrange transportation.
5. Coordinate loading and dispatch.
6. Track delivery where applicable.
7. Confirm receipt at the agreed destination.

Quality & Inspection

Where required, inspection and sampling can be coordinated before loading or upon delivery according to the buyer's agreed requirements.

Delivery Locations

Delivery arrangements depend on the transaction and may include:

- Supplier locations
- Designated warehouses
- Processing facilities
- Buyer-approved destinations

Logistics Partnerships

AMMNIR MINES works with reliable transportation and logistics providers to support mineral supply operations.

Commitment

We aim to coordinate mineral deliveries professionally while maintaining clear communication between suppliers, buyers, and logistics partners.
